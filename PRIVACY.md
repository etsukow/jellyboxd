# Privacy policy

Jellyboxd has no server of its own. It does not collect, sell or share any data, and its author receives nothing from it.

## What the extension stores

Everything is kept in your browser's extension storage, on your device:

- your Seerr URL, sign-in method and Seerr API key;
- your Jellyfin URL and, if you add one, your Jellyfin API key;
- your request preferences: default server, quality profile and root folder, the last ones you used, and the widget style;
- a short-lived cache of the servers and quality profiles your Seerr returns.

## What it reads on Letterboxd

Only on film pages, and only to find the film on your servers: the TMDB id, title, original title, year, director and runtime shown on the page. It does not read your Letterboxd account, ratings, diary or lists.

## Where data is sent

Only to the Seerr and Jellyfin servers you set up, never anywhere else:

- the film's TMDB id, title and year, to look it up;
- your request choices (server, profile, root folder, tags, seasons) when you request a film or cancel a request.

If you sign in with a browser session, your Jellyfin username and password are sent once to your Seerr server to open the session. The extension does not store them; your browser keeps the session cookie for your Seerr site.

There is no analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party service.

## Permissions

- **Storage**: to keep your settings.
- **letterboxd.com**: to show the widget on film pages.
- **Access to your server addresses**: optional, requested when you save your Seerr or Jellyfin URL. Because every server has its own address, the extension can ask for any site, but it only asks for the ones you enter.

## Deleting your data

Click **Reset** in the extension settings, or remove the extension: the browser then deletes everything it stored.
